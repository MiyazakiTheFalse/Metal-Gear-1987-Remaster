# Scope of the prison playtest

The former cell opening concealed the reason Snake accepts capture. The guarded corridor resolved the prototype before the player recovered anything. The original MSX2 script identifies the warden, confiscated equipment and intelligence about Dr. Pettrovich. The J2ME graph and triggers remain open evidence items.

Use the prisoner's intelligence as the opening and let the player surrender at the processing post. Preserve the legacy cell entry for iteration. Move the second breach onto a perpendicular wall, preserve the guarded route, require recovery of the actual loadout, then let Snake cross the exit after the fight. The former browser escort behaviour remains available as an optional variant.

For the deaf or hard-of-hearing route, the changed wall surface, captions, raised weapon and shutter should communicate the same state changes as the foley. Mission design owns first-time comprehension and source comparison. Gameplay engineering owns state and migration. Check the complete quiet route, missed shot, empty-ammo retry, mid-breach checkpoint and post-fight pause before accepting pacing.
