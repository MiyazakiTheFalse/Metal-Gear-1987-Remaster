# Outer Heaven — connective tissue without erasing disorientation

**Design status: proposed doctrine / not an implemented cutscene or established canonical history.**
**Authority order:** 1987 MSX2 events/dialogue > later canon consistency (including MGSV) > documented adaptations > explicitly labelled 3D extrapolation.
**Supreme principle:** It is not what we do, but how we do it.

## Thesis

Build a materially coherent Outer Heaven, and **preserve an epistemically incoherent experience** for a rookie Snake. Buildings, guards, utilities and NPC duties must make operational sense even if Snake never learns what institution really commands them. Each new detail should create a tactile/stealth affordance, inference, emotional beat, spatial orientation or deliberate uncertainty. Avoid cutscenes for things better shown through behaviour.

## Disjointedness to KEEP, DELIBERATELY

1. **Information asymmetry:** Big Boss, resistance contacts, POWs, Fox and guard staff have different and sometimes conflicting knowledge. Logs and environmental observations should not collectively give Snake omniscience.
2. **Spatial uncertainty:** Some routes should branch unexpectedly, conceal their destination, or double back. The world plan must still be consistent. Use landmarks and recoverable mental maps, never arbitrary teleports or indistinguishable corridors.
3. **Uncanny architectural juxtaposition:** A comfortable staffed canteen can share a wall with brutal detention infrastructure. Do not bridge every tonal contrast with exposition.
4. **Radio discomfort and later deception:** Allow a strange order to sit uneasily against what the player sees. Trust can erode through contradiction, but don't spoil the Big Boss reveal or over-signal his motives.
5. **Mechanically surprising encounters:** A boss or locked facility can demand a different tactic, but physical tells and meaningful counterplay should prevent unexplained unavoidable damage.
6. **Silence:** After a rescue or violent firefight, preserve unscored, unvoiced intervals. A constant score, companion chatter and breadcrumb objective feed would erase isolation.
7. **The Venom/Big Boss ambiguity:** Subtle resonance with the later-canon double is valuable. Avoid explicit story exposition, contradictory facial reveals, miraculous recognitions, or new conspirators who lay everything out in advance.

## Disjointedness to REMOVE

- Corridor and camera changes that break the player's understanding of where they are.
- Multiple unrelated keycards or weapons with no believable storage/security logic.
- Guard positions not justified by line of duty, rooms or cover.
- Arbitrary interface overlays, cluttered subtitles or sudden changes to action mappings.
- Story scenes that force unskippable waiting even when the relevant gameplay transaction has already committed.
- Filler hallways, repeated guards for no tactical reason, and lore documents that explain more than a character plausibly knows.

## Level construction protocol

For EACH added room or 3D connector:
1. Annotate its canonical parent beat, source class, and owner/operational purpose.
2. Define how supplies, staff, electricity, prisoners, dogs and vehicles could physically reach it.
3. Give its primary traversal a landmark, visible destination or a discoverable change in elevation.
4. Provide one plausible security duty station and sensory coverage rationale; don't plant enemies purely by enemy-per-minute cadence.
5. Give optional traversal a cost and trade-off (crawl slower, exposed duct approach; guarded main lane faster; backtrack available).
6. Where possible, use an interactive physical clue before any instructional radio hint.
7. Verify 3D camera, full capsule collision, head/hand IK, guarded line-of-sight, cover hitboxes and weapon muzzle collision at 720p.
8. Preserve the ability to retreat, break combat, reload checkpoints and recover an objective without a softlock.
9. Record an original vs adaptation provenance row and a concrete test of what the player must perceive.
10. Treat a proxy, asset pack or draft dialogue as SOURCE ONLY until editor import, compile, runtime QA and novice user tests.

## Experience acceptance

In a blind test, a player should know where they came from, what they can attempt, why a guard occupies a position, and what caused an attack; **they should not necessarily know who is telling the truth, what waits behind the next door, or why the facility's commander seems increasingly contradictory.** This is a desirable distinction between *mechanical clarity* and *narrative uncertainty*.
