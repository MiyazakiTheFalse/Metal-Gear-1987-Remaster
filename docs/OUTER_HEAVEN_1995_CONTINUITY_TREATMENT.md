# OUTER HEAVEN 1995 — CONTINUITY AND CINEMATIC TREATMENT

**Version:** 1.0, 19 November 2026. **Status:** production screenplay direction, NOT complete cinematics, tested mission events or canon dialogue. Contains production-only spoilers for MGSV. **Authority:** Doctrine 000 above Doctrines 002–005 and Snake protagonist bible.

## Our narrative law: two truths, one protagonist

1995 Solid Snake is a new FOXHOUND operative who receives genuine calls from the real Big Boss, investigates Outer Heaven, rescues Gray Fox and finally believes he is confronting Big Boss in person. Later-series canon establishes that the fortress's final opponent is Venom Snake, the real Big Boss's phantom. **Do not allow 1995 Snake to know the MGSV truth.** The audience must feel the original betrayal before any late-series reinterpretation.

**Canon codes:** C87 = original MSX2 supported; CL = later-series canon; I = reversible remake interpretation; U = direct Connect2Media J2ME v3.3.0 evidence pending. Never treat NES alterations as an MSX2 transcript. Every new spoken line needs a C87/CL/I/U source-status label before recording.

## Continuity reconciliations

1. **Two Big Bosses:** real Big Boss commands FOXHOUND and speaks to Snake on radio. Venom occupies Big Boss's identity in Outer Heaven. Behind the scenes they are separate rigs, personalities, performance profiles and assets. In 1995 subtitles and Snake's interpretation both remain simply Big Boss. The ending's original emotional meaning survives.
2. **Genuinely useful instructions:** C87 Big Boss sometimes helps with real tactical details, including confiscated equipment, before giving unreliable routes and eventually an abort order. I: maintaining credibility and expecting failure may explain his early help. Do not assert an official minute-by-minute secret plan or make every early clue a false trap. The same voice and believable personality must carry both good and bad orders.
3. **Rookie becomes legend:** official Konami identifies Operation Intrude N313 as Snake's first mission. His improbable survival and growing capabilities are consequences of player skill and earned tactical information, not a briefing declaring him legendary. His distinctive repeat-a-noun-as-question dialogue should reflect confirmation, disbelief, investigation or dry humour—not cognitive incapacity.
4. **Capture intentionally arranged, not obvious:** C87 a rescued POW points Snake toward the detention route to Gray Fox. I: the player earns entry to the security truck/custody zone, guards discover a believable intruder and detain him with reasonable nonlethal procedure. No invisible fail state that kills him for fighting back; no sentry who seems complicit in a trap.
5. **Gray Fox:** injured but intelligent 1995 human veteran gives bounded intelligence. Do not import the Cyborg Ninja or his later knowledge. His reunion is an emotional beat, not a six-minute information dump.
6. **Schneider:** former Outer Heaven architecture designer and resistance leader, genuinely assists; his later MG2 Black Ninja story does not justify killing him early or giving him instant knowledge of the secret cell.
7. **Drago Pettrovich Madnar:** technical genius coerced by Ellen's captivity. His later allegiances do not prove his original rescue staged. He discloses fifteen sabotage placements, leaving the sixteenth as a player inference.
8. **Ellen Madnar:** capable hostage with personal stakes and a performance history, not a mute plot switch; her safety affects Madnar's willingness to share intelligence.
9. **TX-55:** distinct industrial nuclear weapons platform, guarded by lasers, originally neutralised by 16 correct leg explosive placements. Its engineering destruction and the subsequent encounter with the man Snake thinks is Big Boss are **two separate climaxes**.
10. **Radio cast and aliases:** Schneider, Diane, Steve and Jennifer have finite knowledge and separate dramatic functions. Old MSX2 names (Shoot Gunner, Grey Fox, Arnold, Coward Duck) and later aliases (Shotmaker, Gray Fox, Bloody Brad, Dirty Duck) refer to **the same entities** in respective editions; choose a deliberate localisation policy.

## Snake's repeat-back dialogue: direction, not caricature

Sample I-class **original placeholder** (not source dialogue):
Fox: “They're building a weapon beneath us.”
Snake: “A weapon?”
Fox: “One that changes what this place is for.”
Snake: “Metal Gear?”
The echo is a deliberate request for stakes and meaning, performed with silence and recognition. He may sound incredulous and dry. He must never repeat the name merely to cover an obvious event already shown on camera.

## Production craft rules

- A long scene needs setup, tension, revelation, reversal and aftermath. 2–6 minutes can be appropriate; no fixed quota.
- Scene direction records shot, purpose, actors' knowledge, blocking, eye line, camera choice, audio source, gameplay state and skip/interrupt policy together.
- UE Sequencer only presents committed gameplay events; it never authoritatively confiscates/restores gear or changes mission flags on its own. Skips and checkpoint reloads commit the exact same flags and visual state.
- Mix and match animation libraries **by compatible skeleton, retarget profile, root-motion convention, stride/turn response, clean contact and physical weapon alignment**. A good-looking imported motion is not automatically a production-ready move.
- Every source asset must be licensed for its use, scale-correct, stylistically coherent, visible in 720p gameplay, with performance and animation evidence. No unlicensed ripped Metal Gear assets.
- All actual J2ME-specific room/dialogue claims remain **U** until direct extraction verifies them. All documents remain source/design-only until compiled, captured and tested in Unreal.

## Transaction amendment — prison-to-Shoot Gunner completeness (19 November 2026)

The extended Rust transaction model now distinguishes (1) informant detention-route intelligence, (2) capture and confiscation, (3) Gray Fox and prison escape, (4) Shoot Gunner encounter, (5) gear actually recovered with a **planted transmitter still present**, (6) optional physical transmitter removal, (7) Shotmaker defeat, (8) **Card 3 collected**, and only then (9) slice completion. The existing five-step/capture checkpoint still loads as a compatible starting point; it is not dishonestly described as the new expanded playable opening. The old UE prison scene currently does **not** yet expose all of these new interactions. Source-state logic is distinct from an implemented boss or complete cutscenes.
