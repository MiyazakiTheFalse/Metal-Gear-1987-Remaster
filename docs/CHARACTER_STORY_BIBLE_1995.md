The full specification corrects its principal inaccuracies: **Ellen is hidden in Building One basement**, not Building Three; Snake returns to **Dr. Pettrovich in Building Two** after her rescue; the **TX-55's destruction triggers the countdown before the commander's final fight**; and original Jennifer contact requirements are not the same as optional resistance radio.

## Arc pillars

- Snake grows from inexperienced, trusting FOXHOUND rookie to independently acting survivor.
- Gray Fox's capture, rescue and essential nuclear warning drive the first half of the mission; later cyborg history belongs to future games.
- Big Boss's helpful early guidance makes his false directions and late betrayal consequential; later-canon Venom Snake identity stays behind-the-scenes.
- Schneider's architecture knowledge and private grief, Diane's enemy expertise, Steve's optional Japanese-version radio, and Jennifer's rescue of her brother define the resistance.
- Madnar's coerced weapons development and Ellen's liberation form a tightly linked story that changes the meaning of earlier rooms on revisits.
- Each named mercenary and machine encounter has source-limited character duties without invented biographies.
- Prisoner intel, ranks, physical equipment, Card 8 and the left escape ladder unite narrative and gameplay.
