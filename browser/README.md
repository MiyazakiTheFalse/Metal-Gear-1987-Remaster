# Operation Intrude N313 campaign playtest

**Mission 1 begins at the waterline.** Choose **New Game** to enter Outer Heaven as rookie Solid Snake with his transceiver. Listen to Big Boss, head toward the inspection lamp and scout the supply trucks before looking for the missing operative. Continue restores your campaign checkpoint. The direct custody and Shoot Gunner rehearsal URLs use separate save slots.

The route after the Building 2 tank now connects the maintenance wing, the hostage wing, the TX-55 basement and the surface ladder. Move to the illuminated station and use the normal interaction control. Red and green station lights accompany the sweep and clear-window text. At TX-55, use either physical socket and consult Madnar's sequence in the objective display. The checkpoint remembers each accepted placement.

The later encounters are condensed interaction prototypes. Several original rooms, inventory gates and independently fighting bosses still need implementation. The drainage channel is a remake layout. The story premise follows [Konami's original-game manual](https://metalgear.konami.net/manual/mc1/mg1_msx/pc/en/page02.html).

**21 November — latest playability + architecture pass.** Hold **LB / Tab** for equipment or **RB / G** for weapons, scroll the five-card MGS-style strip with stick/D-pad, mouse wheel or brackets, then release to EQUIP (never auto-use a ration). Press **Y / D-pad Down** to USE the selected item. The scroll model is `equipment_strip.js`, and both game and Unreal code use the same hold/scroll/release contract.

**Outer Heaven interactive architectural blueprint:** while the local playtest server is running, open **http://127.0.0.1:19870/atlas.html** in another tab (or use the title Options link). It displays the proposed whole three-building site and floor stacks, all **52 connected areas**, **114 ledger beats**, **11 arenas**, 55 proposed connectors, named pickups, card gates, elevations and exterior scale. The drawn site is a design proposal, NOT the finished Unreal `.umap` or verified original MSX2 tile placement. See `../docs/OUTER_HEAVEN_MASTER_ARCHITECTURE_1995.md`.

**Local launch:** `Play-Windows.cmd` or `python3 tools/serve_playtest.py`. The local server now serves every committed browser resource, including all original GLBs, cinematics, the equipment model and atlas JSON, rather than silently returning 404 for newly added content.


The campaign now has optional, delayed two-tier anti-stall guidance for bosses, keys, service charges and the tank. Toggle T or the title Options to control hints. If a checkpoint cannot load, Continue refuses to overwrite the unreadable save; use New Operation only after explicitly confirming that you want to replace it. Snake's fatal damage and the pause-menu Retry both resume the last valid checkpoint on a clean page. See [cross-engine playability pass](../docs/CROSS_ENGINE_PLAYABILITY_PASS_2026_11_21.md).

Extract the ZIP. On Windows, open **Play-Windows.cmd** to launch a standalone browser tab on localhost. Keep the launcher window open while playing. The opening screen and pause menu show the connected controller name, live stick values and pressed buttons. Focus the playtest and press A. Directly opening **index.html** also supports keyboard play. Localhost and file-based launches use separate browser checkpoints. To use an existing file-based checkpoint, open the same extracted index.html at its previous location. On macOS or Linux, python3 tools/serve_playtest.py starts the localhost version. Choose **Start infiltration** for the expanded mission or **Continue checkpoint** to resume. Snake, Gray Fox, patrol guards and Shoot Gunner use original skinned models rendered locally in WebGL. The game audio also works offline. The optional character GLB review uses six distinct, locally committed source models (Snake, Gray Fox, guards, Shoot Gunner, Machinegun Kid and the informant), animated from gameplay. Only its Three.js loader/rendering library requires internet. The default WebGL character system still works offline. All of these remain stylised prototype rigs, not MGS4-quality final art.

Move with WASD, look with the mouse, and interact with E. Ctrl crouches, Z goes prone, V enters cover, and F strikes or knocks from cover. Hold the right mouse button to aim and use the left to fire. G opens the weapon selector, brackets select, and releasing G confirms. Hold Tab for items, select with [ / ], release Tab to EQUIP, and press Y to USE the selected item. Equipping a ration will no longer consume it or raise binoculars automatically. C calls on the transceiver; J opens the intelligence log. R safely retries the last checkpoint instead of deleting it.

On a standard gamepad, the sticks control movement and view. A interacts, B crouches, Y goes prone, X strikes, D-pad Down USES the previously equipped item, and the triggers aim and fire. Hold LB/RB to choose equipment and release to equip without accidentally consuming it. View/Back calls on the radio, Menu pauses, and the pause panel lets you Retry From Checkpoint without losing earned state. These bindings match the Unreal 5.7 input source.

The canonical source target is the original 1987 MSX2 game. The Connect2Media J2ME edition is a separate comparison rather than the authority for original item order. This build compresses Building 1 geography. Geometry, dialogue, lighting and timing are provisional. For isolated testing, append ?cell to the file URL before starting. Optional ?escort enables the earlier companion behaviour.


Editable GLBs are included in assets/models in the playtest ZIP. Rebuild them from the browser mesh and motion sources with `node tools/export_field_rigs.cjs`. The committed GLBs now all have 18 joints, including separately articulated jaw and brow. Core full-body clips include idle, walk, run, crouch, prone, down, aim, cover, melee, reaching, hit, radio listen, crouched aim and prone aim; Fox also has a captive performance and Shoot Gunner has four boss-specific performances. Re-export with `node tools/export_field_rigs.cjs` and verify with `node tools/audit_field_glbs.cjs`. Run `node tests/test_animation_intent.cjs` to verify deterministic idle/aim/alert/boss animation priorities, and `node tests/test_campaign.cjs` for world-state and cast-spawn regression. These are stylised playtest models. Unreal import and production character art are pending.

Choose **Rehearse Shoot Gunner** on the opening menu to enter the property hall with recovered gear and a separate checkpoint. Its low cart and tall freight trunks offer different sightlines.

The dispatch yard contains a handgun, a *borrowed* folding freight carton and a marked belt. Hiding in the carton briefly allows an uninterrupted supply-line ride with ordinary staff chatter; this is an optional original remake scene, not the canonical cardboard-box pickup. The original MSX2 cardboard box remains a later, reusable item earned through Card 2 on Building 1's third floor, which is not yet built in this browser prototype. Hold Tab or LB, select a usable item, then release to equip. Cigarettes briefly reveal the nearby optical beam. Capture confiscates your kit until you reclaim it inside the property hall.

After Shoot Gunner falls, use the recovered Card 2 at records stores. Take Card 3, open the east shutter and enter the kennels. The floor passage has a low side duct. Collect the service charge, use it on the patched joint and explore the relay room. After taking the west-lift parachute, approach the actual lift marker to reach the newly connected Building 1 roof. The provisional Hind D has clear approach, warning, strafe and evasive states. Use the roof's blast shelters and the exposed grenade rack to disable it; grenades are finite. The west lift remains reversible with boss progress saved. The roof and helicopter are original prototype art, not a finished or source-verified level. The parachute remains an item, not a scripted teleport trigger. Once Hind D is disabled, use the east roof descent to enter the new Building 2 approach. The tank patrol can be damaged only by player-planted track mines at marked sites; watch the turret telegraph and seek blast shelter. The roof return route preserves planted mines, ammunition and tank progress in V10 checkpoints. The Building 2 end checkpoint and all later encounters remain unfinished. The Machinegun Kid encounter ends at the west-lift parachute. Cleared older saves retain their unlocked exits. An older in-progress loose-card save retains the old pickup.

The processing desk and recovered kit share an audible and captioned two-tone cue. To inspect the kit, hold Tab or LB, cycle items, and use E or A on the unlisted tag to set it down. Releasing the equipment menu keeps it. The boss reacts to individual signals in the property hall. This interaction and its dialogue are remake additions.
