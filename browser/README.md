# Gray Fox browser playtest

Extract the ZIP. On Windows, open **Play-Windows.cmd** to launch a standalone browser tab on localhost. Keep the launcher window open while playing. The opening screen and pause menu show the connected controller name, live stick values and pressed buttons. Focus the playtest and press A. Directly opening **index.html** also supports keyboard play. Localhost and file-based launches use separate browser checkpoints. To use an existing file-based checkpoint, open the same extracted index.html at its previous location. On macOS or Linux, python3 tools/serve_playtest.py starts the localhost version. Choose **Start infiltration** for the expanded mission or **Continue checkpoint** to resume. Snake, Gray Fox, patrol guards and Shoot Gunner use original skinned models rendered locally in WebGL. The game audio also works offline. The comparison checkbox loads the older generic soldier over the network. Attribution is in assets/ASSET_CREDITS.md.

Move with WASD, look with the mouse, and interact with E. Ctrl crouches, Z goes prone, V enters cover, and F strikes or knocks from cover. Hold the right mouse button to aim and use the left to fire. G opens the weapon selector, brackets select, and releasing G confirms. C calls on the transceiver. J opens the intelligence log.

On a standard gamepad, the sticks control movement and view. A interacts, B crouches, Y goes prone, X strikes, and the triggers aim and fire. The bumpers open equipment. Back calls on the radio.

The source target is the Connect2Media J2ME edition. The prison sequence follows comparative MSX2 evidence. The shared campaign uses MSX2 progression as its continuity anchor. This build compresses Building 1 geography. Geometry, dialogue, lighting and timing are provisional. For isolated testing, append ?cell to the file URL before starting. Optional ?escort enables the earlier companion behaviour.


Editable GLBs are included in assets/models in the playtest ZIP. Rebuild them from the browser mesh and motion sources with `node tools/export_field_rigs.cjs`. The committed GLBs now all have 18 joints, including separately articulated jaw and brow. Core full-body clips include idle, walk, run, crouch, prone, down, aim, cover, melee, reaching, hit, radio listen, crouched aim and prone aim; Fox also has a captive performance and Shoot Gunner has four boss-specific performances. Re-export with `node tools/export_field_rigs.cjs` and verify with `node tools/audit_field_glbs.cjs`. These are stylised playtest models. Unreal import and production character art are pending.

Choose **Rehearse Shoot Gunner** on the opening menu to enter the property hall with recovered gear and a separate checkpoint. Its low cart and tall freight trunks offer different sightlines.

The dispatch yard contains a handgun, a *borrowed* folding freight carton and a marked belt. Hiding in the carton briefly allows an uninterrupted supply-line ride with ordinary staff chatter; this is an optional original remake scene, not the canonical cardboard-box pickup. The original MSX2 cardboard box remains a later, reusable item earned through Card 2 on Building 1's third floor, which is not yet built in this browser prototype. Hold Tab or LB, select a usable item, then release to equip. Cigarettes briefly reveal the nearby optical beam. Capture confiscates your kit until you reclaim it inside the property hall.

After Shoot Gunner falls, use the recovered Card 2 at records stores. Take Card 3, open the east shutter and enter the kennels. The floor passage has a low side duct. Collect the service charge, use it on the patched joint and explore the relay room. After taking the west-lift parachute, approach the actual lift marker to reach the newly connected Building 1 roof. The provisional Hind D has clear approach, warning, strafe and evasive states. Use the roof's blast shelters and the exposed grenade rack to disable it; grenades are finite. The west lift remains reversible with boss progress saved. The roof and helicopter are original prototype art, not a finished or source-verified level. The parachute remains an item, not a scripted teleport trigger. Once Hind D is disabled, use the east roof descent to enter the new Building 2 approach. The tank patrol can be damaged only by player-planted track mines at marked sites; watch the turret telegraph and seek blast shelter. The roof return route preserves planted mines, ammunition and tank progress in V10 checkpoints. The Building 2 end checkpoint and all later encounters remain unfinished. The Machinegun Kid encounter ends at the west-lift parachute. Cleared older saves retain their unlocked exits. An older in-progress loose-card save retains the old pickup.

The processing desk and recovered kit share an audible and captioned two-tone cue. To inspect the kit, hold Tab or LB, cycle items, and use E or A on the unlisted tag to set it down. Releasing the equipment menu keeps it. The boss reacts to individual signals in the property hall. This interaction and its dialogue are remake additions.
